What you actually do

A simulation of verbs, not a quiz of buttons.

The room is real and reactive. You examine the patient with your hands, think through an attending physician, and watch the physiology answer you.

Touch

Examine the body

Reach out and check a radial pulse, listen to the chest, open the airway, attach the pads. Each finding reads back from the patient's live state.

Speak

Talk to Dr. Okafor

A voice AI attending coaches you Socratically — "his pressure's climbing and he's clammy, what's your read?" — but never hands you the answer. You state the order and the why.

See

Watch the heart

Call up a living 3D heart that beats at his real rate — ischemic, fibrillating, or squeezed by your compressions — so you can see the consequence of every decision.

Act

Run the code

Start compressions and the camera lifts overhead; call the shock and the pads flash and the body jolts. Do it too early on a perfusing rhythm and the sim stops you.

Reason

Justify every move

Actions without a rationale are flagged. The engine tracks what you noticed, what you understood, and what you did — the way clinical judgment is actually assessed.

Debrief

Read the chart

It ends with a scored incident record: your time-to-treatment against the target, hard-fails, a minute-by-minute timeline, and the diagnosis you were never told.
